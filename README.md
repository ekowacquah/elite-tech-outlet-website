# Elite Tech Outlet — Website Project

Four linked HTML pages for Tech Foundations CIS (CIDM-6303).

## Open the website
Open index.html in a web browser. Keep the four HTML files, styles.css, script.js, and assets folder together. No build step or installation is needed.

## Pages
- index.html: home, product families, and business inquiries.
- products.html: nine real Apple models with category filters, search, official specs, and inquiry links.
- about.html: retail concept and device selection guidance.
- contact.html: validated inquiry download form and FAQs.

## Design and AI assistance
The student supplied the dark home-page design. Codex helped extend that design across four pages, connect navigation, implement interactions, and verify local links and assets.

## Product sources
Apple Support product photos and official model specifications are recorded in assets/sources.json and linked from each product card. Product photography is copyrighted by Apple Inc. and is used here for the educational class project. Apple product trademarks belong to Apple Inc. No affiliation or authorized reseller status is claimed.

## Project limitations
Models are real; this is not live inventory. Prices, condition, fulfillment, warranty, and stock have not been provided. The inquiry form downloads a local text file; it does not transmit data or place orders. No payments or accounts are implemented.
