BEFORE YOU ASK

A few useful answers.

Can I buy a device on this website?

No. This is a student demonstration website, with a sample catalog and no checkout or payment processing.

What should I check when buying a refurbished phone?

Ask about cosmetic condition, battery health, carrier compatibility, activation status, included accessories, and the seller’s return and warranty terms.

What happens to my form information?

The information is used in your browser to create a downloadable text file. This website does not transmit the form entries or save them on a server. Keep the downloaded file private if it contains personal details.
